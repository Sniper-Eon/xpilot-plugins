# Phone SDK guidance delivery and progress clocks

The live v34 session delivered real right/left guidance and camera observations. Phone logs also showed GPS-weak, unmatched-route and stalled-source intervals; those are genuine qualification failures, not proof that C3 lost its network connection. In addition, the source gate deliberately accepts fresh, unchanged, matched SDK callbacks while stationary, but the wire mapper exported the last *changed progress* time as the guidance observation time. C3 independently expires guidance after1500ms. Thus a callback accepted by the phone could still be rejected as guidanceStale by C3.

v35 maps the real SDK callback's monotonic delta from the last progress observation onto that observation's wall-clock anchor. No sender heartbeat/time is used to renew it. Missing callback clocks retain the legacy timestamp; backwards, future and overflowing clocks cannot manufacture freshness. Neither the stored progress timestamp nor NavigationSourceGate's movement/progress/recovery checks changes. Real GPS/route qualification losses and the C3 phone-location age/accuracy checks remain enforced; this change does not claim to repair them.

Failure-first regression expected a genuine unchanged SDK callback to export7000ms while retaining progress2000ms/elapsed100ms, and failed on the old mapper. A later sender heartbeat must still export7000ms. Invalid callback clocks retain2000ms. Final Android suite:369tests, zero failures/errors, one skipped (explicit hardware-only activation). The app APK builds; an initial incremental packaging failure cleared on a packaging-only rerun without source changes. Original package/signature and all28 model/native members remain identical to v34. No new host hook, message field, C3 plugin, rule or firmware is required; upstream developer document/API revisions were rechecked and unchanged.
